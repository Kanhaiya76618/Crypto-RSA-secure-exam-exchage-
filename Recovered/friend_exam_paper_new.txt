ENGINEERING FACULTY — CONFIDENTIAL EXAMINATION PAPER
=====================================================
Course   : CS3501 — Cryptography and Network Security
Date     : 29 October 2026
Duration : 3 hours
Max Marks: 100

                        PART A  (5 × 2 = 10 Marks)
                        ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
1. Define the Avalanche Effect in block ciphers.
2. State Kerckhoffs' principle.
3. What is the difference between a stream cipher and a block cipher?
4. List two properties required of a cryptographic hash function.
5. What is the purpose of a Message Authentication Code (MAC)?

                        PART B  (5 × 6 = 30 Marks)
                        ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
6. Explain the Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange protocol with an example.
7. Describe the RSA key-generation algorithm step by step.
8. Compare ECB and CBC modes of AES. Why is ECB insecure for images?
9. Explain the working of HMAC with a diagram.
10. What is a digital certificate? Describe the X.509 structure.

                        PART C  (3 × 20 = 60 Marks)
                        ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
11. Design a secure file-transfer protocol that provides confidentiality,
    integrity, and sender authentication. Justify each cryptographic
    primitive you choose.
12. Implement the ElGamal encryption scheme in Python. Encrypt and
    decrypt the message m = 42 with a 256-bit prime.
13. Analyse the security of SHA-256. Discuss birthday attacks and their
    implications for digital signatures.

                            --- END OF PAPER ---
